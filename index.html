The shift

From writing the page to owning what happens next

For about seven years I was a B2B SaaS content writer. I was good at the part of marketing that lives on the page: keywords, articles, the content calendar.

I kept hitting the same limit. I could write the piece, but I didn’t own what happened after it went live. I was also bored.

So I moved into marketing and growth, where you own the whole picture. Content used to be my whole job. Now it’s one part of a system. Search brings people in, the page converts them and the data shows what to do next.

I’m still early in growth work and learning fast. Seven years of writing taught me what buyers want to read.

Operating system

How I think about growth

Three principles. Built by testing, breaking things and keeping what moves the number.

01

Growth isn’t a marketing channel.

It’s how a business wins its market. Content, positioning and SEO can’t live apart. When the whole system talks to itself, pipeline compounds.

Systemic approach
02

AI takes execution. Humans take the risk.

AI is taking over drafts, research, reporting and repetitive SEO. That makes the human part matter more. Someone still has to take chances, control the narrative and decide where to bet.

The human edge
03

Test. Break. Fix. Keep what works.

I run small experiments all the time. Not everything works, and that’s the point. The only edge that lasts is iterating faster than everyone else.
